# Privacy Policy – Cowboy Duel

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

This policy explains what data the Cowboy Duel app ("the game") collects.

**Who is responsible**
Ejnar Games (sole proprietorship), CVR 42350915, Møldrupvej 1E, 8752 Østbirk, Denmark.
Contact: slagmarken.games@gmail.com

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The game does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

The game saves a few settings (for example which cowboy you picked and whether you have seen the intro) only on your own device. They never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

App distribution and any payments are handled by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies.

## Children
The game does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights
Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing to see, correct or delete. For any questions, email slagmarken.games@gmail.com. You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), www.datatilsynet.dk.

## Changes
If the game starts collecting data (for example if online play is added), we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released.
