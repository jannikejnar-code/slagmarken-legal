# Privacy Policy – Funny Drunk

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

This policy explains what data the game Funny Drunk ("the game") handles, why, and what you can do about it.

**Who is responsible for your data**
Ejnar Games (sole proprietorship), CVR 42350915, Møldrupvej 1E, 8752 Østbirk, Denmark.
Contact: jannik@ejnargames.dk

## The short version

Funny Drunk has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Most of what the game remembers stays on your phone. Only Play with Friends sends anything to our server, and that is deleted automatically.

## What we handle and why

| Data | Where it is | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your nickname, chosen character, audio settings and the number of free matches you have used | Only on your device | To remember your choices between sessions | Not sent to us |
| Play with Friends: your nickname, chosen character, a random session ID, your answers, response times and scores, and the room code | On our room server while the room exists | To run a shared match between you and your friends | Contract |
| Your IP address during Play with Friends | Processed by our server provider when you create or join a room | To deliver the connection and to limit abuse | Legitimate interest |
| Purchases of "Unlock Unlimited Matches" | Handled by Apple | To unlock the full game and restore your purchase | Contract |

Play with Friends rooms are deleted automatically about 24 hours after they are created. Your nickname is only shown to the players in the room you create or join.

We never see your payment card or your Apple Account details. Apple tells the game only whether the unlock has been bought.

We do **not** collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or advertising ID, we show **no ads**, we use **no analytics**, and we **never sell** your data.

## Who helps us run the game

- **Cloudflare** hosts the Play with Friends room server and its database.
- **Apple** handles downloads and purchases through the App Store.

These companies may process data outside the EU/EEA under the safeguards they provide, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Children

The game has a drinking theme and is not directed at children.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you can object to our processing. Because the game has no accounts and room data is deleted within about a day, we will usually have nothing stored about you. You can delete everything stored on your phone by deleting the app.

To use your rights, write to jannik@ejnargames.dk. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.dk.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
